Context (phase 2, f923 'Splits'): an accepted split of taxon A changes each affected Gall's Inducer and nothing else. Galls the splitting paper itself places settle on its daughter; each other Gall on A gets one question (:split_inducer), 'Which taxon induces this Gall now?'.

Chosen: a split's daughters may include A itself, in its narrow sense. The question's options are each daughter, A in its narrow sense, or 'not known yet' (the Gall stays on A, marked as following the name in its old broad sense: relationship_facts.broad_sense_split_id, shown on the Gall page).

Why: the usual split ('A into A and B') keeps the name A for part of the old concept; f923 describes splits that way. Requiring every daughter to be a new taxon would force a fake new taxon for the part that keeps the name.

Alternative: daughters must all differ from A (A retired by the split).

Code: Taxa.split/3, Import.SplitQuestions (answering A itself keeps the Gall on A, unmarked), Catalog.repoint_inducer/4, Catalog.mark_broad_sense/4, the :split_inducer queue kind and its question page.
