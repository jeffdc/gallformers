What was decided. /galls (GallsBrowseLive) and /hosts (HostsBrowseLive) moved onto the browse design Jeff approved on 2026-11-05 (he called it "so much better"). Each page is a grid of family_card (DomainComponents), whose toggle opens a family across the grid to its genera, each with a caret listing its Galls or plants.

The tree's behaviour is kept:
- the search form ids (galls-browse-search-form, hosts-browse-search-form), field query, 300 ms debounce, search on change and submit;
- toggle_node with the same f-/g- keys, expand_all and collapse_all;
- smart_expand's thresholds (3 families, 5 children);
- the leaf links, the empty messages, the lazy undescribed tree and data-active-view.

The changes, logged under "Approved changes" in docs/design-system/inventory/domain-and-media.md:

1. The undescribed toggle is a checkbox ("Undescribed galls only"), sending the same toggle_undescribed. Jeff's round-1 feedback: "Undescribed is just a filter on the inducer view."

2. A family or genus whose own name matches the filter keeps everything under it.
- Before: with the old filter_tree/2, a matched family lost its genera and became a bare link.
- Now: BrowseHelpers.filter_tree/2 (with filter_cards_tree/2) has these card semantics on all three Browse pages, /places included (it was first left unchanged for /places, then moved over when /places was rebuilt).

3. /hosts orders plant families by distinct Galls (Catalog.host_gall_counts/0), most first, instead of by name. Families with no Galls stay, last. Jeff's round-1 feedback asked for a host view by Host family, "sorted by count like the inducer view".

4. Both pages have the h1 "Browse galls" and the Browse tab_nav (BrowseHelpers.browse_tabs/0). "Expand All | Collapse All" became "Show all galls" (or plants) and "Collapse all".

5. /galls groups families into plain-language groups (Gallformers.Catalog.InducerGroups, proposed content; unknown families go in "Other"). Its sections carry #group-<slug> anchors, so the home page's "Browse by what causes them" links (/galls#group-<slug>) land on them.

The alternative is to keep the old tree's behaviour: the toggle as a button, a matched family as a bare link, /hosts by name, the old headings and controls.
