cb28 open question 12. The situation: decided with Jeff, an import is credited to a database Source per day of access ("iNaturalist, accessed 8 Nov 2026"), dated that day, made on the day's first import and reused after, so an import competes by the day it was made. For non-plant taxa each placement, name, synonym and common name is that Source's taxonomic statement, accepted at once by the curator importing; plants are edited directly with no statements, marked origin `:inaturalist` and keeping their iNat ID. The plan didn't give the Source's fields, or whether the new mapping table is public.

What was built: the day's Source (`Sources.inaturalist_title/1`, `inaturalist_source/1`, `create_inaturalist_source/2`, made and logged once a day by `Import.record_inaturalist_source/2`) is kind database, author/authors "iNaturalist", link and URL https://www.inaturalist.org, no license set. An animal import that adds nothing makes no Source. The `taxon_inaturalist` table is listed public in `priv/snapshot/tables.txt`, like `taxon_wcvp`, so the public snapshot carries the taxon ↔ iNat ID mapping; the catalog migration clears it and the replay log makes it again.

The alternative: set a license on the Source (cb28 names none), or keep `taxon_inaturalist` out of the public snapshot.

Why the conservative choice: cb28 gives one reason for all its open questions: where the plan was silent, the conservative choice was taken, and each is easy to change. The table's visibility follows `taxon_wcvp`'s, as cb28 says.

Context: cb28 'Add a taxon from iNaturalist' is built on taxogall (e69af545). From a review question, and from the taxonomy admin's New Taxon and edit pages, a curator finds a taxon on iNaturalist, sees it beside our tree, ticks what to bring in (the taxon, the ancestors needed to attach it, its children, its names) and adds it in one change credited to iNaturalist. The panel is `inat_taxon_panel` (`GallformersWeb.ReviewComponents.inat_taxon_panel/1`, its state in the LiveComponent `GallformersWeb.Admin.InatTaxonPanel`); what it offers and what it writes are `Gallformers.Import.InatTaxa.plan/2` and `carry_out/3` (as of a0434bcf; b230 Task 7 moves the component to its own module). Jeff approved the design and mockup on 2026-11-08. Where they were silent the builder took the conservative choice, and cb28 says each is easy to change. These are the built defaults Jeff is to settle while checking cb28 on real data (package 119's Rubus Host, Floate's Syrphidae).
