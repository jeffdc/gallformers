Context (phase 2, f923 'Lumps'): an accepted synonymy of B into A needs no questions. B's Galls get A as Inducer with their Facts and attributions (each keeping its name_as_written), their names update through the reviewed rename flow, and B is redirected and deleted.

Chosen: in the lump, B's taxonomic-act Facts move onto A's Facts with the same key (a placement under B becomes one under A; a synonymy into B one into A), attributions untouched. B's own placement and name statements thereby become statements about A. Each of those from a real Source that would count, other than one confirming A's current placement or name, is declined with the reason 'B was merged into A' and kept as history. The Gallformers Sources' statements of B (Gallformers' own and the legacy data, which count without acceptance and can't be declined) are removed; the audit keeps them. A statement that would place A under itself is removed.

Why: once moved onto A, B's old placement and name would compete in A's slots and could move A or rename it; declining keeps what each paper said, visibly, without letting it change A. Deleting them would lose the literature's record.

Alternative: delete B's statements with B, or leave them counting on A (letting B's old position move A).

A lump is refused for a B with children, for plants, or while an open question names B.
