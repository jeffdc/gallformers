What was decided, and why. Made when the rank pages moved onto the design system (2026-11-05), within the higher-taxa design Jeff approved that day: tabs, pagination, and the plant genus and Fagaceae pages. Recorded under "Approved changes" in docs/design-system/inventory/rank-pages.md. The pages are /genus, /section, /family, /subfamily, /tribe and /subtribe/:name.

1. The Galls' related-genus filter became the Host genus facet.
- Before: a collapsed "Show filters" panel of related genera (toggle_related_genera, filter_related_genus) filtered the Galls table.
- Now: Host genus is a facet beside plant part, form and season. It allows several choices, and it is offered only from 8 Galls up and only with 2 or more options. That threshold is the round-2 mockup approval: "facets only at 8 or more Galls".
- Code: GallformersWeb.RankGalls (@min_for_facets 8, @facets) and RankPageComponents.rank_galls.
- On plant pages, the plants' related-genus filter moved to the rail (inducer_genera_panel): each Gallformer genus filters the Species table, with "Show all plants" to clear.

2. The family and tribe Galls tab lists the whole subtree.
- Before: the page showed only the Galls placed directly at the taxon. Those now show as Undescribed.
- Now: every Gall in the subtree, loaded by RankGalls.load(%{inducer_family_id: id}) through Catalog.identify.
- /family now routes to IntermediateLive :family, and FamilyLive was deleted. The taxa within are a second tab (?tab=within).

3. A plant family's genera table counts the Galls on each genus.
- Before: it counted Galls induced by the genus, which is almost always 0. That count only makes sense for mistletoes.
- Now: the Galls on each genus, from Catalog.host_gall_counts/0.
- Code: RankPageComponents.plant_table and children_list (plants: true).

4. The always-zero Hosts column is gone.
- Before: on the family and intermediate children tables, "Hosts" counted plants in the subtree (Taxa.count_plants_under/1: Plantae at species rank or below), not Hosts used. On a Gallformer family it was always 0, and the tests pinned the zeros.
- Now: Gallformer children are a dense column list with Gall counts (sort "Most galls" / "Name"), in RankPageComponents.children_list.

The alternative is to keep the old behaviours: the collapsed related-genera filter, Galls placed only at the taxon, Galls-induced counts and the Hosts column. bc0d recorded no reason to keep them beyond parity.

Not part of this entry: unknown names on rank pages are now 404s (GallformersWeb.NotFoundError). That is the separate question on 200 vs 404.
