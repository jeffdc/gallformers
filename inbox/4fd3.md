Background (matter 803b, decision 7, Jeff 2026-10-26): the old `taxonomy.description` on genera, sections and intermediate ranks, mostly common names like "Elms", became common names of the taxon (74 carried over; family descriptions were dropped). These were flagged afterwards (803b, rehearsal of 2026-10-27) as doubtful, and not blocking. As held on gallformers_taxogall (taxon_common_names; old taxonomy row in brackets):
- "Creeper" on the genus Parthenocissus [taxonomy 199]
- "Plane" on the genus Platanus [586]
- "Plums, Cherries, and Allies" on the genus Prunus [208]
- "Rowans and Mountain Ash" on the genus Sorbus [227]
- "Aspens and White Poplars" on the section Populus [997]
- "necklace or bigleaf poplars" on the section Leucoides [1169]
- "Bramble, cinquefoil, and rose inquiline gall wasps" on the tribe Diastrophini [1458]

803b doesn't say why each is doubtful. On their face, two are bare or partial names ("Creeper", "Plane"), and the others put several common names, or a description of a group, in one string.

What hangs on it: a taxon's common names show on its rank page, and global search finds a taxon by them (`Taxa.search/2` searches accepted names, synonyms and common names). Each name can be kept, reworded, split into separate common names, or dropped. Matter 803b records no recommendation. It doesn't say whether the fix goes in the migration (the descriptions are carried over by the migration from the old taxonomy table) or is an edit after cutover; Diastrophini is not a plant, and in phase 2 a non-plant's common name is taxonomy: its first common name raises a taxonomy question (50ff, 'For Jeff's review').
