Background: a Trait (plant part, timing, minor form…) is a Fact about a Gall, keyed Gall + Trait + value. The question is whether a Trait can apply only on a particular Host, e.g. 'petiole on Q. rubra, leaf on Q. velutina'.

The domain model (docs/domain/domain-model.md, 'Being discovered') leans no: a real host-dependent difference goes in a Source's statement, or justifies a separate Gall entry. The model already supports that: a Gall name can carry a Host qualifier (host_qualifier_taxon_id, one of the stored name parts), as in host-scoped forms such as '(on betula)'. The question still needs a definitive answer.

Options:
- No (the lean): no change. Host-specific forms are separate Galls, and Sources' host-dependent remarks stay in their quotes.
- Yes: Trait Facts gain an optional Host qualifier. That changes:
  - the Trait Fact's natural key and the import contract (traits.json and the producer and checker guides);
  - the standing rules (is 'leaf' on Gall X the same Fact as 'leaf on Q. velutina'?);
  - the ID tool's Trait filters, which today match per Gall, and the Gall page display.

No recommendation beyond the domain model's lean is recorded.
