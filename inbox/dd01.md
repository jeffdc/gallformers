Context (f14c 'The Source's Facts after approval', agreed with Jeff 2026-11-04; built as the admin corrections page, /admin/sources/:id): a correction to a Source's applied Facts never edits a Fact (a Fact can hold several Sources' attributions). The curator withdraws the Source's attribution where the Source didn't say it, and asserts what it did say (another Host, Trait value, parent or accepted taxon), carrying over its words and evidence, with a required note. Corrections are audited, listed, move the Source's Updated date and replay (:source_correction). Under the unified edit model (d3b0) this is fixing a misreading under the Source's authority, at its date.

Chosen: when the corrected statement is taxonomic (a parent or accepted taxon), the new statement awaits a curator like any other unaccepted taxonomic statement, unless the curator ticks 'Accept it now', which accepts it in the same step (a :taxonomic_decision replay entry after the :source_correction).

Why: phase 2 counts a taxonomic statement only once accepted (f923); a correction asserts a new statement of the Source, so it starts unaccepted, and the tree doesn't move until someone accepts it. 'Accept it now' saves the second step when the correcting curator also follows the paper.

Alternative: a correction inherits the acceptance of the statement it replaces, or is always accepted at once.

Code: Import.SourceCorrections, the fact_editor component (FactsComponents), Import.TaxonomicDecisions.
