Background (matter 8861; b5bd 'Plants kept twice (2026-11-08)'). Some v1 plants exist twice, as two `species` rows with Galls hosted on each, and usually one row's name is also an alias of the other. The catalog migration turns plant aliases into synonyms (`aliases.csv`, rule 4). The resolver then quietly picks the accepted name, so a Source naming either plant links to only one of the two rows: the Galls hosted on the other go unmatched, and the name lookup hides that the plant exists twice.

How they were found: after a rebuild, look for a plant name that is accepted on one taxon and is also a synonym with no sensu on another, then check each name against WCVP. Numbers in parentheses are the Galls each plant hosts; IDs are the old species IDs, which plant taxa keep.

WCVP says each pair is one plant:
1. Cupressus nootkatensis 2160 (0 Galls) into Callitropsis nootkatensis 1271 (1).
2. Cupressus pigmaea 2236 (1) into Hesperocyparis pygmaea 1268 (2). "pigmaea" is a misspelling, and WCVP doesn't hold it.
3. Fallopia japonica 4728 (1) into Reynoutria japonica 1529 (1).
4. Grindelia robusta 3384 (1) into G. hirsutula 3181 (5).
5. Alnus viridis 36 (2) into A. alnobetula 5308 (1). WCVP treats A. viridis as A. alnobetula subsp. alnobetula, so this is a species against a subspecies.
6. Populus trichocarpa 4539 (12) and P. tristis 249 (12). WCVP sinks P. trichocarpa into P. tristis. 8861 calls that unexpected: it needs checking against other authorities before deciding. No direction is recorded.

Precedent: Jeff decided two such pairs on 2026-11-08, following WCVP. Silphium asteriscus var. dentatum 4863 went into S. dentatum 4878, and Horkelia congesta 5190 into Potentilla congesta 4870. Both are in `priv/catalog_migration/plant_merges.csv` (committed in 0071d788).

The options, per pair:
- Merge: one row in `priv/catalog_migration/plant_merges.csv` (`species_id, into_species_id, note`; the note says why and who decided, as the existing rows do). The catalog migration reads it in step 11c (`Gallformers.CatalogMigration.PlantMerges`) and runs the editorial plant merge (`Gallformers.Import.Merge`), unlogged, once Facts, names, notes and images exist. The absorbed plant's names become synonyms of the plant kept; its Host Facts, notes and images move to it; its ID redirects to it; and it is deleted. Both rows must be plant `species` rows, which all twelve are. A curated file is the only way a v1 fix reaches the cutover; a merge done on a local database does not (b5bd M6).
- Keep both: nothing changes, and the silent pick described above stays.

Open points: for pair 5, whether to merge a species into one that WCVP holds at subspecies level; for pair 6, the outside check and the direction. The scan only finds this one pattern (a name accepted on one plant and a synonym on another). Duplicates where neither name appears on the other row, an infraspecific row duplicating its species with no alias, or a misspelling with no alias are not covered, and nobody has estimated how many there are.
