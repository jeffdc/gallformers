cb28 open question 7. The situation: decided with Jeff, the ranks above the nearest one we hold are folded into one unticked line ("Plantae › … › Rosales · 5 ranks above it we don't hold · Show them"). Ranks we don't use are skipped in the lineage, which leaves open whether the count includes them.

What was built: the count includes the ranks we don't use (as the mockup did). With one or two ranks the line reads "Rosales" or "Magnoliopsida › Rosales".

The alternative: count only the ranks we use, the ones that could be added.

Why the conservative choice: cb28 gives one reason for all its open questions: where the plan was silent, the conservative choice was taken, and each is easy to change. Here it also follows the mockup Jeff approved.

Jeff approved the design and mockup on 2026-11-08. Where they were silent the builder took the conservative choice, and cb28 says each is easy to change.
