Context (Ranges.WcvpAuthorship, f14c 'Built 2026-11-07'): plants take their names' authorship from WCVP. Every WCVP link write goes through Ranges.Wcvp.put_link/3, which calls WcvpAuthorship.follow_link/3: adding a plant from WCVP, a match, a sync, and the replay log.

Chosen: replaying a WCVP link change (at cutover, preview's log into production) reads WCVP afresh to fill authorship. The replay entry doesn't carry the authorship that was written on preview.

Why: one rule produces plants' authorship everywhere, from the WCVP data Gallformers holds, and blank names are filled by the same lookup.

Consequences: the replay's result depends on the WCVP data in the target database matching preview's. If WCVP can't be read, follow_link does nothing and silently leaves the names blank (Release.backfill_wcvp_authorship/0 can fill them afterwards). The replay must start WCVP (a dev-rehearsal bug, fixed in dff79486).

Alternative: log the authorship value in the link change's replay entry and replay that value, so the replay doesn't depend on WCVP.

Code: Ranges.Wcvp.put_link/3, Ranges.WcvpAuthorship.follow_link/3, Import.Replay, Release.replay_log/1.
