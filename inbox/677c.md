Context (f14c 'Undo rule', Jeff 2026-11-05: acceptable): Undo of a decided review question is allowed only while nothing depends on it: the Source isn't approved yet (cross-Source questions, such as taxonomy acceptances, split answers, rename proposals and same-date questions, never hold approval, so this part doesn't apply to them), and nothing the decision changed has been changed since, per the audit log (Carbonite). Otherwise Undo says who changed what and when, linking to the taxonomy admin or the Gall. Undo reverses what the decision did and reopens the item with its notes; replay kind :queue_decision_undone. 
Chosen: Undo refuses, with the reason in words:
- decisions made before decisions recorded their tree edits: undo reverses exactly what the decision logged for replay ('carried_out' on its :queue_decision entry); an older decision has no such record, so what it changed can't be known ('Can't undo: this decision was made before decisions kept what they change…'); split answers decided before 90a55ef0 are among these;
- accepted splits: they re-pointed Galls (each affected Gall then has its own split question); reopening the acceptance wouldn't put them back;
- lumps: B was merged into A, redirected and deleted;
- Trait-term decisions that changed the vocabulary (placing a term for every Source, marking it no-fit, and so on): a term placement applies across every Source, so only a 'keep' decision (no vocabulary change) is undone here; the vocabulary page (/admin/vocabulary) changes the rest;
- questions closed as history: undone through the newer statement's decision.

Why: each refused kind either lacks the record of what to reverse or changed things beyond its own item, where an automatic reversal would be wrong or incomplete.

Alternative: build reversals for these kinds (e.g. un-lump by recreating B, move split Galls back), or a compatibility path for pre-record decisions (see the open question on split undos on preview).
