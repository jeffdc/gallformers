cb28 open question 10. The situation: the import records each taxon's iNaturalist ID in a new `taxon_inaturalist` table (taxon_id primary key, `inat_id` unique, audited, deleted with the taxon), beside `taxon_wcvp`, through a replay-logged edit `{:set_inat, taxon, id}` (e1d2's mapping). The plan said the iNat ID is set on every taxon added or matched; it didn't say what happens when one side is already linked, or whether a link can be removed.

What was built: links are set on the taxon, every lineage rank held or added, and every child added or moved, unless either side is linked already (a unique iNat ID per taxon). On a taxon's edit page, a taxon iNat links to another of ours is refused in words. Unlinking isn't offered.

The alternative: replace an existing link, link only the taxon itself, or offer unlinking on the edit page.

Why the conservative choice: cb28 gives one reason for all its open questions: where the plan was silent, the conservative choice was taken, and each is easy to change. cb28's stated rule: a unique iNat ID per taxon.

Context: cb28 'Add a taxon from iNaturalist' is built on taxogall (e69af545). From a review question, and from the taxonomy admin's New Taxon and edit pages, a curator finds a taxon on iNaturalist, sees it beside our tree, ticks what to bring in (the taxon, the ancestors needed to attach it, its children, its names) and adds it in one change credited to iNaturalist. The panel is `inat_taxon_panel` (`GallformersWeb.ReviewComponents.inat_taxon_panel/1`, its state in the LiveComponent `GallformersWeb.Admin.InatTaxonPanel`); what it offers and what it writes are `Gallformers.Import.InatTaxa.plan/2` and `carry_out/3` (as of a0434bcf; b230 Task 7 moves the component to its own module). Jeff approved the design and mockup on 2026-11-08. Where they were silent the builder took the conservative choice, and cb28 says each is easy to change. These are the built defaults Jeff is to settle while checking cb28 on real data (package 119's Rubus Host, Floate's Syrphidae).
