Elsewhere (b5bd M0, M6; 9449 §5), preview is the persistent staging server: curators' decisions are made there and reach production only through preview's replay log, exported at cutover (`Release.export_replay_log()`). Running a full `Release.migrate_catalog()` on preview's own database during the backscan would rebuild the catalog that curators have been working in. The rebuild never touches `replay_log` (9449 Task 17), but it empties the new domain tables and the import tables, queue items included (`Gallformers.CatalogMigration` moduledoc and `@clear`, as of a0434bcf). So the curators' work would come back only by replaying preview's own log. The matters don't say how the two fit.
