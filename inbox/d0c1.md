cb28 open question 3. The situation: the panel lists the taxon's children on iNaturalist ("Under it on iNaturalist", the first 8, held ones first, "Show N more"), all unticked; decided with Jeff: one we hold under another parent can be moved under it ("we have it under Rubus · Move here"), one we don't hold added. The alternative: cb28 names none; the evident one is a checkbox with a rank picker, as the taxon itself gets (open question 2).
