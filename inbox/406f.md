Context (bc0d's admin migration had hand-built a 'recommended' chip; the component build, commit 836fbe65, made it a chip variant): a review question's options are review_option cards built on radio_card. Each card shows the option's label, its tags (chips such as :best or :conflict) and a 'Recommended' chip on the recommended answer.

Chosen: the Recommended chip comes after the tags. In radio_card (FormComponents) the chip follows the label slot, and review_option puts its tags in that slot.

Note, as of a0434bcf: review_option's newer `use: true` card (the question card) renders a 'Suggested' chip before the tags. The two variants disagree in order and wording.

Alternative: the chip directly after the label, before the tags.

Code: GallformersWeb.FormComponents.radio_card, ReviewComponents.review_option (both variants), the chip :recommended variant; the catalog entries in docs/design-system/components.md (radio_card, review_option).
