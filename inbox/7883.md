Context (phase 2, f923): an accepted split re-points Galls from A to its daughters through per-Gall curator answers; an accepted lump of B into A moves B's Galls, with their Facts and attributions, onto A, and B is redirected and deleted.

Chosen: when the paper behind an accepted split or lump is retracted (or its statement declined), the moved Galls stay where they are. For a lump, the synonym goes but B's Galls stay on A. Moving them back ('splitting back') is curator work.

Why: f923 records it as 'splitting back is curator work'. As built, a lump deletes B (redirected), and a split's moves are per-Gall curator answers, so an automatic reversal would have to recreate a deleted taxon or overturn curators' answers on its own.

Alternative: on retraction, re-point the Galls back automatically (restore A or B from the recorded moves).

Code: Facts recompute on retraction, Taxa.Sync (follows slot winners: the synonym is removed), Taxa.Lump / Taxa.merge/3, Import.TaxonMerges, Import.SplitQuestions.
