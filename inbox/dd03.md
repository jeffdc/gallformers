Background: Undo of a decided review question (Import.Undo) reverses exactly what the decision logged for replay. Before the fix in 90a55ef0 (merged 2026-11-07; d6e78fdd), a split question's answer didn't record what it moved ('carried_out' via SplitQuestions.moved/5, reversed by a 'return_inducer' op). So:
- undoing a split answer decided before the fix is refused ('Can't undo: this decision was made before decisions kept what they change…');
- an undo of a split answer that was performed before the fix is in the replay log as a :queue_decision_undone entry without the reverse moves, so at cutover it replays without moving the Inducer back. Production would then keep the Gall on the daughter while preview has it back on the split taxon.

This matters only if split undos ran on preview, whose replay log is what reaches production. Decisions made on local databases never reach the cutover.

How to check: on preview, look in replay_log for :queue_decision_undone entries keyed to :split_inducer decisions, dated before 2026-11-07.

Options:
- (a) none ran: nothing to build;
- (b) some ran: add a compatibility path in the replay (e.g. derive the moves from the audit trail), or redo those undos by hand on production after the replay;

Related: f465 (2026-10-23) says preview has had no Postgres of its own since the main Postgres cutover. The preview-environment phase provisions one and loads a fresh production copy. If whatever earlier preview data exists isn't kept, no earlier preview undo can reach the cutover, and the answer is (a).
