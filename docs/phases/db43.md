# Review cleanup

This phase gives one way to handle a name we don't have, clears what the 2026-11-09 review audit found, and runs the import review's last checks: its keyboard, and Jeff's walk-throughs on real data.

## Why

The new import review (one Source at a time, one question at a time) was built quickly, between 2026-11-06 and 11-08, and it outran its plans. Jeff's walk-through on 2026-11-09 found that a name we don't have is handled differently on every question. The Host question offers "Search…" and "Add a new plant ↗", which do the same job. The related-taxon question (a parasitoid, say) looks and works like a different screen.

Two read-only audits followed. One mapped every unknown-name path; the other looked for duplicated or dead code across the review, iNaturalist-import and Fact-inspector work. They found:
- two question UIs on one back end;
- two WCVP searches and two iNat searches;
- old step and Facts pages still routed;
- dead code;
- copied helpers and test fixtures.

This phase fixes that. It also takes over the old pages' retirement from the review build. The guardrails that now stop such duplicates (duplicate-code check, one entry point per capability, retirements, the commit review) are already in. This phase's work is what those guardrails were given as a shrink-only backlog.

## The mechanic we want

Every question about a name we don't hold (or can't place) offers the same answers in the same order:

1. **The suggestions**, if any: "Use X", marked Suggested or Best guess, with the same identity line everywhere.
2. **Find it…:** one search, results grouped.
   - In Gallformers (for a Host, genera and sections too).
   - WCVP (plants only; picking one adds it from WCVP).
   - On iNaturalist (picking one opens the iNat panel; its Add answers the question).
3. **Add it by hand…**, in place: the name, its rank and its parent (the genus filled in when we hold it). For a plant at species rank or below, its Host range too. This replaces the "Add a new plant ↗" link.
4. **Leave it out.**

A common-name Host keeps its two extra answers: "A section of X" and "Only the genus".

Behind them sit one answer builder, one search, one decision shape and one carry-out, run inside the decision. Whatever the decision creates (from WCVP, from iNat, by hand) is created when the curator decides and logged with the decision. So Undo takes it back, and a refused decision leaves nothing behind.

**Where it applies:**
- the Host questions;
- which taxon is it (`:called_new`, `:not_held`, `:several_species`);
- the synonym questions;
- a Host dispute read as another plant;
- a hedged Host statement's "Add it…";
- the missed names' "Add a statement…".

Outside the review, the New Taxon page and the Fact inspector's plant pickers use the same search.

**Real differences, kept as settings (not separate paths):**
- Plants are editorial; other taxa are Source statements (the Source under review, the day's iNaturalist Source, or the basis picked on the New Taxon page).
- WCVP is for plants only.
- A common-name Host never becomes a synonym and may resolve to a genus or section.
- A Host reading records the Source's sense of the name (sensu).
- A Host above species rank goes in as `host_taxon_id`, with no reading.

**The interfaces:**
- **The decision shape** for a name: `{taxon_id}` | `{wcvp_id}` | `{inat: %{id, ticks…}}` | `{new: %{name, rank, parent_id, range?}}` | `{skip}`, plus `sensu` for a reading. `Import.Queue` accepts it for `:unresolved_host`, an ambiguous Host, `:unresolved_name` and the `:ambiguous_match` kinds.
- **The carry-out** runs inside `Import.decide_queue_item/3`'s transaction and branches on kingdom:
  - plants go through `Import.TaxonomyAdmin` / `InatTaxa.carry_out/3`, editorially, with the Host range required at species rank or below;
  - other taxa become statements of the Source under review, or of the day's iNaturalist Source;
  - everything is logged in the decision's `carried_out` entry for `Undo.reversal/1`, and replayed by `Import.Replay`.
- **The search:** `GallformersWeb.Choices` is the one place that runs and words a name search. It provides `wcvp_plants/2`, `inat_taxa/1`, the Host choices, the parent search, the meta line, `trait_values` and "readers' values first", and one place search.
- **The answers:** one builder in `GallformersWeb.Admin.Review.Questions`, with settings `kind: :plant | :taxon`, `common_name?`, sections and genus. `Choice` carries a `leave?` flag.
- **The spelling:** one helper. The Source's spelling, its abbreviation expanded, becomes a synonym for every name decision except a common-name Host. There's also one "do we already hold it" check, and `Rank.parse/1`.

## Jeff's decisions (2026-11-09)

1. **A plant added by hand at species rank or below needs its Host range**, asked for in place, as the New Taxon page does.
2. **A new non-plant taxon is created when the curator decides**, credited to the Source under review, not later at apply time.
3. **The Source's spelling becomes a synonym for every name**, Host or not (a common-name Host still never does). An earlier idea to exempt OCR errors is dropped.
4. **Retire `ItemRow`:** the notes page, the taxonomy queue and Renames move onto the new card.
5. **Dead code goes.**
6. **The iNat panel in the review hands its choice back to the card.** Its Add no longer imports at once: the chosen taxon (with its ticks) becomes the answer, and the import runs inside the decision. So a refused decision leaves nothing, and Undo takes the import back (until now, Undo reopened the question but left what the panel had brought in). On the New Taxon page it still imports at once.
7. **Everything in this cleanup is done here,** including the parts in the Fact inspector's code: its pickers, its photo picker, its reading-fix checks and its missing-input wording. Splitting them off would be the same fragmentation this phase fixes.
8. **The notes page's "Needs you" tab draws each held note's questions as the Everything view's rows** (`review_question_row` around `QuestionCard`), so a question looks the same everywhere.
9. **The Facts edits go on the review's last step:** its Facts list (`facts_view`) gets the old Facts page's per-Fact menu (Drop, Change Host, Change value, Note), with the shared `fact_editor` under the Fact and Undo under each edit. (The review mockup doesn't draw these.)

## Earlier decisions that still govern the review (Jeff, 2026-11-08)

- **The mockup and these decisions are the design authority.** The old review pages never shipped: they were a test bed for working with the data, and none of their interface behaviour is a contract. The design-system rule to keep every behaviour when replacing a shipped component doesn't apply to them. Where the mockup is silent, ask Jeff rather than carry over what the test bed did.
- **A Source is authoritative for what it says.** Every statement goes in credited to it, including ones that disagree with what we have. The review asks only what Gallformers can't tell (which plant, Gall or term) and what the pipeline's AIs disputed. A candidate Gall is always one click; its comparison is evidence, not statements to settle. That's why the settle follow-up is dead code.
- **About 90% of edits come in through ingestion and this review.** The Fact viewer and editor is a separate tool for on-the-fly edits, and the two are not merged. Tools the review needs to settle a question in place (the iNat taxon import, adding a plant) are reusable components with the review as their main caller. The review's own edits (the last step's statement edits) are built in the review.
- **The Facts ("what approving will add") are the last step before Approve,** by Gall, and reachable from the rail at any time.
- **Undo is on the last answer** ("Saved: … Undo" on the next card). Older answers are undone from the Everything view. Refusals show in place with their text and link.
- **No Reject button:** unticking in a batch is enough.
- **Answers needing input say "Search…" for a name and "Choose…" for a value.**
- **Keyboard, a small set:** Enter takes the suggested answer, S skips, up/down move between answers.

## What the audit found, by design concern

- **A. One name, many paths.**
  - A taxon was created five ways: a link out to the New Taxon page for a Host, a parent picker created at apply time for a non-Host, the synonym split at decide time, the New Taxon page, and the iNat panel importing outside the decision.
  - The back end refused `new` for an unresolved Host but accepted it for an ambiguous one.
  - Suggestions were built twice. The WCVP search was written three times and the parent search twice.
  - The spelling-and-synonym helper was written three times, with three different rules for expanding an abbreviated genus.
  - The duplicate check was written three times.
- **B. Old pages still running.** The step pages (`ReviewLive.Step`) and the Facts page (`ReviewLive.Facts`) are still routed with nothing linking to them, along with the components only they use and about 3,000 lines of tests. The dry run is written twice, and one copy crashes on an error.
- **C. Two question UIs.**
  - `ItemRow` still runs the notes page, the taxonomy queue and Renames.
  - The Trait answer, the note box and Undo exist twice, with different decision shapes and behaviour.
  - Old wording ("Recommended", "To check") is still live.
  - The iNat hint shows where iNat isn't searched.
- **D. Dead code:** the settle follow-up and a handful of unused functions and components.
- **E. The shared search isn't shared:** two wrappers of the Host choices, two meta lines, two place searches, three `trait_values`.
- **F. iNat duplicates:**
  - two iNat searches that differ in minimum length, "matched" test and wording;
  - copied number formatting, taxon-name italics (losing the upright "var."/"×") and refusal text;
  - iNat URLs built in several places, and two builders of the same result map;
  - mismatched parse shapes;
  - the panel reaching past the `Import` boundary.
- **G. Design system:**
  - raw link buttons and hand-rolled panels;
  - "Names we didn't read" drawn as the hedges card;
  - the section heading written inline twice;
  - an answer recognised by its wording;
  - the Fact inspector's photo picker copying another component's grid.
- **H. Tests:** copied fixtures and helpers, and "Postgrex … client exited" noise from async work tests don't wait on.
- **I. The same thing defined two ways:**
  - two reading-fix checks and two reading-fix spec builders;
  - three missing-input wordings;
  - the hedged sections listed three times;
  - counts computed in two places;
  - `inat` naming three things;
  - catalog and mockups docs out of date.
- **J. Waste:** a per-entry reload of each missed name's context, and the whole notes page built to read a Source's kind.

## Guardrails this phase works under

- **One entry point per capability** (`SingleEntryPoint` in `.credo.exs`).
  - The WCVP name search, the iNat taxon search and the Host choices are owned by `GallformersWeb.Choices`.
  - Review decisions (`Import.decide_queue_item/3`, `decide_group/3`) are owned in the web layer by `ReviewLive.Answer`.
  - `Taxa.create_taxon/2` is owned by `Import`, `Import.ApplyTaxonomy` and `CatalogMigration.Taxa`.
  - Today's other callers are listed as pending on this cleanup: `ReviewLive.Answer`, `TaxonomyLive.New`, `TaxonomyLive.RangeEditor`, `InatTaxonPanel`, and `ReviewLive.Review` (batch Accept).
  - The check fails when a pending caller no longer makes the call, so each change that moves a caller removes its pending entry. The list only shrinks; owners change only by Jeff deciding to.
- **Retirements** (`test/support/retirements.exs`). A task that replaces a module adds an entry first, as its failing test, and deletes the old module in the same commit. While an entry is outstanding nothing else can be committed. `ItemRow`, `ReviewLive.Step` and `ReviewLive.Facts` are each replaced by `ReviewLive.Review`.
- **Shrink-only baselines.** The duplicate-code baseline (`mix gallformers.guardrails.baseline`) and the design-system conformance baseline (`mix gallformers.design_system.baseline`) are lowered after each cleanup to lock the gain in. They are never raised.
- **Every task:** write the failing tests first and watch them fail, then build. `mix precommit` clean. A task that replaces something deletes it in the same task. Each task starts in a fresh session. Commits go through the reviewed commit gate.

## Order, and why

Items in a phase are unordered, but the work has an order:
1. **Dead code first.** It's a day or less and shrinks what everything else touches.
2. **Then the back end, the search and the answers, in that order.** The answer list needs both the decision shape it decides with and the search its "Find it…" runs.
3. **Then the old pages.** Retiring the step and Facts pages and retiring `ItemRow` can go in either order.
4. **Then the small cleanups:** the iNat helpers, the design-system fixes, the tests, and the remaining definitions and waste. They go in any order once `ItemRow` is gone.
5. **The keyboard after both retirements,** so it's built on the settled card.
6. **The real-data walk-throughs last,** once everything above has landed.

The guardrails' pending entries clear as the search and the decision get their single owners.

## The keyboard

A `ReviewKeys` hook on the question card. Enter takes the suggested answer, S skips, and up and down move between answers. It's inert while typing in a field. The server events are tested in LiveView, and the hook has its own JS unit test; it's checked by hand too (an e2e test only if cheap). The palette's global `/` and `s` keys must keep working beside the review's S.
