# Ranges

All range work lives here (Jeff, 2026-11-16). The model is in the plan doc's *Ranges* design principle and the decision log (items d178 and 7128); this phase builds it.

## What it builds

- **Places on PostGIS:** our ISO places, TDWG regions and named areas in one model, with boundary polygons; any place resolves to the leaf places it covers, and a point maps to places.
- **Host ranges as Facts:** WCVP's statements in its own regions, native or introduced; a curator's correction is a Gallformers statement. Replaces the 1.08M-row `host_range` expansion and today's five range editors.
- **A Gall's predicted range:** its Hosts' native range, narrowed by its *only within* and *absent from* Facts (per Gall, by place). An introduced region counts only with evidence.
- **A Gall's observed range:** occurrence Facts, a point (with uncertainty, date and cited observation) or a place only, as most literature gives it.
- **Matching:** the ID tool and place pages use predicted and observed, and say why a Gall matched. The ID tool takes non-leaf places, and the first-visit Region prompt is replaced. An occurrence that conflicts with a restriction still counts, and goes to a curator.
- **No Gall without a range:** every Gall has a Host and every Host a range, enforced by the writers; today's gaps cleaned up.
- **The migration's ranges:** Host ranges from the WCVP links, the old site's restrictions and its curated introduced places as legacy data.
- **The screens and curation** that item 7128 settles.

## Done when

Ranges are stored and read as above, the `host_range` expansion is gone, the ID tool and place pages match on predicted and observed, and no Gall can be without a range.
