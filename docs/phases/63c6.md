# Gall relationships

## Why

The model and the import contract hold all 15 kinds of Relationship (`docs/domain/domain-model.md`, *Relationships*; `Gallformers.Catalog.RelationshipTypes`; `priv/import/vocab/relationships.schema.json`), but the data has almost none: 8,289 Host links, 3,691 Inducer links and one "emerged from" on dev (2026-11-12). The Gall page doesn't show a Gall's community, and nothing beyond a Host or Inducer can be added by hand. Jeff, 2026-11-12: this needs its own place in the plan.

The screens are accounted for, designed and built on stubs in *The UI*; this phase makes Relationships real behind them, on *One write path*'s writers and *One editor*'s connected screens.

## Rules

- **The Gallformers Source is a real Source** (Jeff, 2026-11-15). A Relationship a curator adds under it is treated like any other Source's statement: dated, attributed, and outranked by a newer Source.
- **Every Relationship happens in a Gall** (plan doc rule 8). Types and qualifiers are a fixed system list. Hyperparasitism is derived from chains of *parasitoid of*, never stored.
- **Alternate generations are stated on their own evidence**, never inferred from a shared Inducer (domain doc). The system may propose likely pairs, but a pair is linked only when a Source states it, a curator's confirmation under Gallformers included. Mismatches are flagged (plan doc rule 8).

## Done when

- All 15 kinds can be read and written through their one writer, with their qualifiers; a Gall's community shows on its page, and an organism's Relationships show on its species page; hyperparasitism is derived.
- Likely alternate-generation pairs are proposed for a curator to confirm, and mismatches are flagged.
- The producer reads Relationships from Sources reliably, checked against the golden Sources.
- The "modified form of" cases found by *Migration data*'s inquiline audit are stated.

Sources: the domain doc; the 2026-11-12 history audit (state/4-data-model.md); Jeff, 2026-11-12 and 11-15.
