# Public organism taxon pages

Design public pages for non-plant organism taxa after the gall-centered multi-species model (`2cb2`) is implemented. The foundational feature intentionally shows taxon identity, authorship, common/scientific names, roles, confidence, and associated taxa within gall pages only. This follow-up should determine whether identity-only taxon pages with associated galls improve navigation without expanding Gallformers into a comprehensive inducer-biology database.
