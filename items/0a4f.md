Public pages for non-plant organism taxa: identity-only pages for Inducers and other organisms, with the Galls associated with them.

The idea (from 2026-08, before taxogall): design public pages for non-plant organism taxa, as a follow-up to the gall-centered multi-species model. That earlier foundational feature showed taxon identity, authorship, common and scientific names, roles, confidence and associated taxa only inside Gall pages. The open question was whether identity-only taxon pages with associated Galls improve navigation, without expanding Gallformers into a comprehensive inducer-biology database.

Since then, on taxogall (checked at a0434bcf), the new back-end has public pages for non-plant taxa:
- `/species/:id` (`SpeciesLive`, merge a2d8440a): non-plant taxa at species rank and below. Plants keep `/host/:id`, higher ranks go to their own pages, and merged taxa redirect. It shows:
  - a header with the name, authority, rank, lineage and common names;
  - every Gall the taxon induces, all generations together;
  - its taxonomy: current placement and accepted name with their Sources, synonyms, papers that disagree, and split and lump history;
  - its Galls' ranges together on one map, and its Sources;
  - Edit mode through "Based on which Source?".
- Rank pages: `/genus/:name`, `/family/:name`, `/section/:name`, `/tribe/:name` and the other intermediate ranks.

So what is left to triage is whether anything beyond these pages is wanted, for example pages for associates that induce nothing (Parasitoids, Inquilines) reached through Relationships. If nothing is, this capture can go.
