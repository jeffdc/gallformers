**docs/architecture/range-system.md.** Written for the species-keyed range model:
- **"Two Range Tables"**: `host_range` and `gall_range`, keyed by `species_id`. `host_range` is now keyed by `taxon_id`; see `Gallformers.Ranges.HostRange`, which still carries a `species_id` field until the old columns are dropped. The curated `gall_range` table no longer drives a Gall's range.
- **"Gall Range Resolution Order"**: `Ranges.get_display_range_for_gall/1` and the `gallhost` fallback. That function is gone. A Gall's range is now the potential range from its Hosts' ranges plus its range Facts (exclusions); see `Gallformers.Catalog.ReadModels.PotentialRange` and `Catalog.Facts.RangeFact`.
- **"WCVP Integration → Data Flow"** and **"Duplicate Place Entries in Sync"**: `Ranges.update_host_places/2`, now gone. `Ranges.update_taxon_places/3` exists.
- **"Sync Pipeline"**:
  - `Plants.load_sync_ref_data/0` and `Plants.compute_powo_diff/3` are now in `Gallformers.Ranges.Wcvp`.
  - The "Host Edit page" is now the taxonomy admin's range editor; `/admin/host-range` still exists.
- **"Gall Range Curation"**, the whole section:
  - the implicit-exclusion model on `gall_range`;
  - `/admin/gallhost` and its map colours (now a redirect);
  - `gall_traits.range_confirmed`, `Galls.invalidate_gall_ranges_for_host/1`, `/admin/gall-range` and the "Needs review" badge.

  Range exclusions are now edited from the Gall page's Edit mode (`GallLive.RangeEdit`) into the change tray as Fact items. `range_confirmed` now lives on `Gallformers.Ranges.TaxonWcvp` (a Host's range, not a Gall's).
- **"Boundary Dependencies"**: `Gallformers.Galls` → `Ranges` → `Places`, `Species`.
- Still accurate: "DisplayRange" (`Ranges.compute_display_range/2`), "Places Hierarchy" (`Places.batch_leaf_descendant_ids/1`, `list_all_places/0`), "Map Tiles" (`services/boundaries/`) and "TDWG-to-ISO Mapping" (`Wcvp.Tdwg.convert_tdwg_codes/2`). Re-check the details as you go.
