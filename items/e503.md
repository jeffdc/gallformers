Take `vocab_requests` out of `Gallformers.Import.Undo`'s `@machinery` list. The table no longer exists.

## Background (as of a0434bcf)

- **The table:** `vocab_requests` was 6611's vocabulary backlog (`20261102180000_create_vocab_requests`). It was replaced by the superadmin vocabulary page (d213). Migration `priv/repo/migrations/20261106200000_drop_vocab_requests.exs` drops it and its audit trigger (matter f14c, Housekeeping).
- **The list:** `@machinery` in `lib/gallformers/import/undo.ex` (about line 123) holds "the import's own tables, worked out again by an undo: a later change to them depends on nothing the decision changed". It still lists `vocab_requests`.
- **Its one use:** `edited_since/1` passes it as `$2` to its Carbonite query. Changes to these tables after a decision don't count as later edits that block the undo.
- **Keep the `vocab_requests_decided` replay kind.** It is in `lib/gallformers/import/replay.ex` (moduledoc: "no longer written, and not replayed") and `lib/gallformers/import/replay_entry.ex` (the kind list and its type). The drop migration keeps it allowed on purpose: old `replay_log` entries are kept and skipped. Leave the `replay_log.kind` constraint, `/admin/vocab-requests` → `/admin/vocabulary` redirect (`AdminRedirectController.vocab_requests/2`) and the create migration alone.

## Edge case to check first

The Carbonite trail (`carbonite_default.changes`) may still hold `vocab_requests` rows written before the drop, in any database that ran both migrations (dev, test or rehearsal copies). In `edited_since/1`, the first branch of `later` matches later changes to the *same rows* the decision changed, for tables not in `@machinery`. Once `vocab_requests` leaves the list, a decision made between the two migrations that also wrote a `vocab_requests` row could find a later change to that row and be refused as edited.

- Check whether such rows can exist.
- If they can, decide whether that refusal is right, and test it. `describe/2` has a catch-all clause ("a record it changed"), so it won't crash.
