Seed the Trait-term table from producer output, and have a curator (a superadmin) rule on the ~850 existing (Source wording, value) pairs **before go-live**, so that early Sources don't flood the review with term questions.

Why it matters: trait values are categories, and each Source's own words are sub-terms placed under them (d213). A term is placed automatically only when it is already in the term table, or when the producer and the checker independently place it in the same category. Disagreement, or either reader saying nothing fits, makes it a curator question. With an empty table, nearly every term in the first Sources would be a question.

How (the tool is built; checked present at a0434bcf):
- `mix gallformers.import.term_candidates <dir>...` (`Import.gather_term_candidates/2`, system actor `"term_candidates"`) gathers the Trait terms of producer packages (each directory is searched for `gall.json`) into `trait_term_candidates`. Gathering a package again replaces its counts. A decided candidate is never changed. Terms the table already holds are reported, with the categories producers suggested other than theirs. Example: `MIX_ENV=dev PGDATABASE=<db> mix gallformers.import.term_candidates services/import-producer/output/golden-2026-10-27 …`.
- "Seed terms" on `/admin/vocabulary` (superadmin) places a candidate, marks it ambiguous, or marks it no-fit, singly or in bulk where the uses agree.

Rules the ruling must keep (d213):
- **A term goes under one category, or under each value its words name** when they name several (Jeff, 2026-11-03, replacing "one category per term"): "protruding from both sides" → upper leaf + lower leaf; "green-yellow" → green + yellow. Guessing between values is never placement: an "or" or an unstated side is `leaf` ("on the leaf" → `leaf`; `leaf` is a real value, implicit in every leaf value, and stored only when the Source supports nothing more precise). Otherwise the term is decided Gall by Gall.
- **A term used in more than one sense across Sources** (e.g. "swelling": abrupt, tapered or stem club, depending on the gall) is marked ambiguous and never mapped automatically.
- **Placing a term that fit no category** turns its no-fit text uses into Facts. A use a curator chose to keep as text stays text.

Do it **on preview**, where Sources are processed. Term placements replay to production as `:trait_term_edit` entries, while seeding done on a local database doesn't reach the cutover. `trait_term_candidates` is private in the public snapshot. Done when the candidates gathered from the current producer output are all decided on preview, before Sources are processed at scale.

The tool exists (d213); the curator work isn't done. From matter d213 ('Consequences', 'Seeding', Task F and its status).
