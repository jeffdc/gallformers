Rewrite `docs/domain/admin-onboarding.md` (the "Gallformers Admin Guide") for the admin as it stands on taxogall, then walk Adam through it so he can process Sources on preview on his own. This is the curator onboarding step before processing Sources into the production copy.

The doc was last changed 2026-11-06 (337855ca), before most of the curation and phase 2 work. As of a0434bcf it is stale in at least these places:
- **Gall editing:** it sends admins to **Galls** (`/admin/galls`, `/admin/galls/:id`) with per-section saves. Those pages are gone: they 301 to `/galls` and to `/gall/:id?edit`. Gall editing is now Edit mode on the public Gall page, with the change tray (Review and apply, with evidence asked once per change set) and the Fact inspector (Gallformers' actions, and "Fix our reading…" for a Source's misreading).
- **Vocabulary:** it describes **Vocabulary** as "the vocabulary requests". That backlog is gone. `/admin/vocabulary` is the superadmin page for Trait categories and Source terms: spot checks, terms fitting no category, add a category, move a term, mark it ambiguous, and "Seed terms".
- **Missing entirely:**
  - the taxonomy queue (`/admin/review/taxonomy`: accept or decline a paper's taxonomic statement, and "Accept the rest of this paper");
  - split questions;
  - "Based on which Source?" on non-plant taxonomy edits (plants stay editorial);
  - the Source corrections page (`/admin/sources/:id`);
  - Undo of a decided question, and when it is refused;
  - the changes page (`/admin/changes`) and the dashboard's recent changes;
  - Add from observation;
  - the ⌘K-style palette (keys `/` and `s`);
  - the review Facts page (`/admin/review/sources/:id/facts`);
  - Gall merges and deletes with their impact checks;
  - the `/species/:id` page.
- **The "Old links" paragraph** should match the redirects in the router (`AdminRedirectController`).

The Source intake procedure itself is `runbooks/source-intake.md`. The guide should point to it rather than repeat it. The edit-model phases will still rebuild the editors onto one framework, so check the doc against the admin as it is when Adam starts.
