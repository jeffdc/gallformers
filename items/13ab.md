Three rank-page findings from the rank-page inventory (`docs/design-system/inventory/rank-pages.md`, "Findings" and §4). They were left open when the rank pages moved onto the design system. The pages are `/family`, `/subfamily`, `/tribe`, `/subtribe`, `/genus` and `/section/:name`.

- **Case-sensitive lookups.** Lookup is exact on the accepted name and the route's rank: `Taxa.get_by_name/2` in `lib/gallformers/taxa/read_models/browse.ex`, `n.name == ^name`, reached through `GallformersWeb.RankPage.lookup/2`. So `/genus/andricus` is not found, while `/genus/Andricus` is. Since bc0d, not found is a 404 (`GallformersWeb.NotFoundError`), so a wrong-case link is a hard 404.
- **Synonyms don't redirect.** Only accepted names are looked up. A synonym in the URL is not found (404) rather than redirected to the accepted name's page. Numeric old IDs do redirect, through the crosswalk.
- **Dead routes.** `/infratribe/:name`, `/supertribe/:name` and `/infrafamily/:name` are still routed to `IntermediateLive` (`router.ex`, about lines 242–244). Those ranks aren't in `Gallformers.Taxa.Rank.all/0`, so the pages can never show anything. Before bc0d they rendered "Taxonomy not found" (a 200). Now they always raise `NotFoundError` (a 404), per `IntermediateLive`'s moduledoc and `mount/3`.

Checked in code as of a0434bcf. The inventory has related findings that this capture doesn't cover (not rechecked):
- Family, section and intermediate pages silently take the lowest ID when names are duplicates.
- The genus disambiguation offers "Family Unknown", a dead end.
- Old numeric URLs redirect with a 302, not a 301.
- Every all-digit name is treated as an ID.
