A Fact left `:unsupported` by an undecided conflict is hidden everywhere else on the public site. But the Source page (`/source/:id`, "What this Source says") lists it publicly with an "unsupported" mark: `fact_mark` in `lib/gallformers_web/components/facts_components.ex` shows the raw standing ("withdrawn", "superseded", "retracted", "unsupported"), titled "This Fact's standing in Gallformers".

As of a0434bcf, `lib/gallformers_web/live/source_live.ex` renders `facts_view` from `Import.facts_for_source/1` without its `history` option, which collapses Facts that aren't current under the rest. So unsupported Facts are still listed (read in code, not checked in a browser). `facts_components.ex` counts `:unsupported` among the past standings (`@past_order`).

Related: the same section and the Galls list disagree after a retraction (capture "Source page: a retracted Source's Facts stay listed…").

Details: `docs/design-system/inventory/host-and-source-pages.md`, "Findings" ("Unsupported Facts are public here").
