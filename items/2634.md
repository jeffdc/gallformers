Implement cynipid phenology in Gallformers: a public phenology explorer, and timing estimates on Gall pages. This is implementation work, not an investigation of Adam's standalone Phenology tool (github.com/Megachile/Phenology).

**Starting point:** Megachile's draft PR #578 (branch `phenology-release`, 14 commits, last 2026-09-08, cut from main at 6e816fb9).
- Implementation details live in the PR rather than in the matter.
- The PR is an unreviewed draft, not an approved design or a ready-to-ship commitment. Its approach and code need review before implementation resumes.

**Why it waited:** implementation was deferred until the taxonomy/model direction was resolved, so as not to build against a model that might change. That direction is now taxogall: the species tables are replaced by Galls, taxa and Facts.

**What the branch holds** (`git diff --stat main...phenology-release`, 44 files, about 8,000 lines):
- **Data:** a `Gallformers.Phenology` context (`observation.ex`, `prediction.ex`, `seasonal_clock.ex`, `blacklist.ex`), and migration `20260517170909_add_phenology_tables`.
  - `phenology_observations` is keyed by `species_id` and `host_species_id` (`references(:species)`), with iNat IDs, raw and normalized phenophase, date, day of year, latitude and longitude, life stage and viability.
  - `phenology_blacklist` is keyed by `inat_id` and `species_id`.
- **Pages:**
  - `PhenologyLive`, the explorer, with `phenology_filters.ex`;
  - `PhenologyController`, CSV export;
  - `phenology_components.ex` and `phenology_chart_data.ex`;
  - a lazy phenology panel added to `GallLive`;
  - router and layout entries.
- **Reference data:** `priv/phenology/seasonal_landmarks.csv` and `README.md`.
  - The CSV is a fixed, species-independent latitude reference (WorldClim 2.1 normals, 25–55°N) compiled into `SeasonalClock`. Serving predictions needs no climate downloads and no R or Python process.
  - Predictions pool species within a generation. They weight each occupied 1° cell equally, centre the year with a weighted circular mean, and take fresh-gall onset as the earliest developing record on the latitude-normalized clock: an earliest *recorded* onset, not a confidence bound.
- **Tests and fixtures** for each.

**On taxogall it needs re-keying:**
- The PR's tables reference the old `species` table, and `GallLive` has since been rebuilt on the design system. So the data has to move onto Galls and taxa (and the Gall page's new layout) before it can land.
- bc0d dropped a home-page "In season now" section because the site lacks per-visitor data and abundance. That's a sign of what the Gall-page estimates should and shouldn't claim.

Megachile's draft PR #578 (branch phenology-release, 14 commits) is the starting point and is waiting.
