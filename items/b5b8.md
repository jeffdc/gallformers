Three Host page (`/host/:id`, `lib/gallformers_web/live/host_live.ex`) findings, from `docs/design-system/inventory/host-and-source-pages.md`, "Findings":

- **Galls on the plant's genus never appear on its species' pages.**
  - The page's Galls are those with a current `host_of` Fact on exactly this taxon (`RankGalls.load(%{host_id: id})`). Galls whose Host is the genus (the old "Quercus spp." placeholders, which became the genus taxon as Host) appear only on the genus page's "Galls on the genus as a whole".
  - Likewise, a variety's page doesn't list its species' Galls, nor the species page its varieties'.
  - Partly addressed since: the rebuilt Host page has an `#on-genus` line, "N more galls are recorded on *Genus* as a whole, with no species named", linking to the genus page. The Galls themselves still don't show (as of a0434bcf), and nothing covers varieties and species.
- **A variety's breadcrumb omits its species.** In the inventory, `LineageBreadcrumb.from_lineage/1` kept only family, intermediates, genus and section, so a variety's page never showed or linked its species, which has its own `/host/:id`. The rebuilt page builds its crumbs in `HostLive.crumbs/1`: every ancestor that `TaxonomyURL.public_path/1` gives a page. That goes through `Taxa.TreeBuilder.rank_path/2`, which has pages only for family, subfamily, tribe, subtribe, genus and section, so the species ancestor is still left out (read in code, as of a0434bcf).
- **The old `/host/:id?source=` deep link is ignored.**
  - The link: e3010f6f added `/host/:id?source=<id>` to open that Source's description.
  - How it broke: 67378c93 dropped plant Sources (803b decision 1) and the `handle_params` with them.
  - So inbound links with `?source=` load the page and silently drop the parameter. Today's `handle_params` reads only `?edit`.
