**What:** clean up the iNaturalist helpers the audit found copied or drifting (finding F, other than the search, which is item e7ef's). Use the shared helpers in place of copies. Build one iNat URL module and one result map. Shape `parse_taxon_id` like `parse_observation_id`. Read WCVP from the panel through `Import`. Give the panel component what it shows instead of letting it call `InatTaxa`. Move `inat_taxon_panel` into its own components module, and name the three `inat` things apart. This is b230's Task 7. It's small and can go in any order after item f926 (retiring `ItemRow`). References are as of `65531d59`; go by function names.

**Why: what the audit found (finding F).**
- **Copied helpers:**
  - `inat_observations` (`review_components.ex` about 2844-2853) copies `GallformersWeb.Helpers.format_number/1`.
  - `@inat_italic`/`inat_italic?` (review_components.ex about 2377, 2833) and seven inline italic spans (`<span class={inat_italic?(…) && "italic"}>`, as of `a0434bcf` in `inat_taxon_panel`'s rows, plan head, lineage, children and additions) re-implement `taxon_name` (`data_display_components.ex` 45). They lose its upright "var." and "×".
  - `InatTaxonPanel.refusal/1` (`live/admin/inat_taxon_panel.ex` about 206-228) re-implements `GallformersWeb.Admin.TaxonomyLive.Errors.text/1`, and leaves "%{count}" unfilled in its changeset messages.
- **iNat URLs are built in several places:**
  - taxa: `inaturalist/taxa.ex` about 140 and `taxonomy_live/edit.ex` about 899;
  - observations: `inaturalist.ex` about 115, `curation_components.ex` about 324 (`observation_url`), and `FactsComponents.observation_href`/`observation_name` (`facts_components.ex` about 1797-1807), which copies `CurationComponents.observation_url`.
  - A grep for `inaturalist.org` (as of `a0434bcf`) also finds a search link in `ui_components.ex`, observations-by-Gallformers-code links in `gall_live.ex` and a people link in `profile_live.ex`. The audit didn't name those.
- **Other duplicates:**
  - `INaturalist.Taxa.result/1` and `taxon_result/1` build the same map (`inaturalist/taxa.ex` about 72-94).
  - `INaturalist.Taxa.parse_taxon_id/1` returns `{:ok, int} | :error`, while `INaturalist.parse_observation_id/1` returns `{:ok, string} | {:error, :invalid_input}`.
- **Boundary drift:**
  - The panel reads WCVP itself, with `rescue _ -> []` (inat_taxon_panel.ex about 189-204). That's `wcvp_names/1`: the accepted names of WCVP's records of a name, offered as one-click searches when iNat has nothing. It gets the lookup through `Application.get_env(:gallformers, :wcvp_lookup, …)` and calls `find_names`/`get_names`.
  - The function component calls `InatTaxa.counts`/`additions`/`rank_choices` directly (review_components.ex about 2558-2566).
  - `inat_taxon_panel` lives in `ReviewComponents`, though the New Taxon page uses it too.
- **`inat` names three things (finding I, done here per Task 7):** `Choice.inat` (`admin/review/choice.ex` about 98), the Answer/QuestionCard state `inat: true` (answer.ex about 67, question_card.ex about 61) and Review's `inat` assign (review.ex about 72). New Taxon also has `inat_open` (new.ex about 75, 260), and Answer sends `{:inat_open, …}` (answer.ex about 176).

**Build:**
- Use `format_number/1`, `taxon_name`, and `TaxonomyLive.Errors.text/1` in place of the copies.
- One iNat URL module, used by every place above; one result map; `parse_taxon_id` shaped like `parse_observation_id`, with `{:error, :invalid_input}` on bad input. Update its callers (`Import.parse_inat_taxon_id/1` and the taxon edit page's "Link to iNaturalist").
- The panel's WCVP read goes through `Import`, with specific rescues in place of `rescue _ -> []`.
- The component no longer calls `InatTaxa`: the LiveComponent (`GallformersWeb.Admin.InatTaxonPanel`) passes in what it shows.
- Move `inat_taxon_panel` to its own components module, and update its catalog entry in `docs/design-system/components.md` and its style-guide entry.
- Name the three `inat` things apart.
- Rank parsing here (`InatTaxonPanel.rank_of/1`, `InatTaxa.our_rank/1`) belongs to `Rank.parse/1`, item 7378.

**Calls:** `GallformersWeb.Helpers.format_number/1`, `DataDisplayComponents.taxon_name/1`, `TaxonomyLive.Errors.text/1`, `Import` (for the WCVP read).
**Deletes:** `inat_observations`, `@inat_italic`/`inat_italic?` and the seven inline spans, `InatTaxonPanel.refusal/1`'s copy of `Errors.text`, the iNat URL copies (`FactsComponents.observation_href`/`observation_name`, `CurationComponents.observation_url`, the inline taxa URLs), one of `result/1`/`taxon_result/1`, the panel's direct WCVP read, and `inat_taxon_panel` in `ReviewComponents`.

**Tests (first, watched failing):** an iNat taxon at variety rank or a hybrid renders through `taxon_name` with an upright "var."/"×". A refusal with a count reads filled in. `parse_taxon_id`'s new shape. The panel's WCVP fallback still offers names, and a lookup failure is handled by a specific rescue. Run the existing `inat_taxon_panel_test.exs`, `review_live/inat_test.exs` and `taxonomy_live/inat_test.exs` throughout.

**Done when:** each helper above has one definition and the copies are deleted. `inat_taxon_panel` is in its own module, and the component calls no `InatTaxa` function. `mix precommit` is clean, and the duplicate-code baseline is lowered (never raised) if findings dropped.
