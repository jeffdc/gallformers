**What:** retire the old review pages: `GallformersWeb.Admin.ReviewLive.Step` (the five step pages) and `GallformersWeb.Admin.ReviewLive.Facts` (the Facts page). Before they go, move the Facts page's per-Fact edits and its dry run onto the new review's last step. Then redirect their URLs into the review. This is b230's Task 5 (finding B), and it absorbs 6611's Task 10 ("Retire and redirect"). It runs after the one answer list (item bceb). It and item f926 (retiring `ItemRow`) can go in either order. References are as of `65531d59`; go by function names.

**Why (finding B, "old pages still running").**
- `ReviewLive.Step` (373 lines) and `ReviewLive.Facts` (600 lines) are still routed (router.ex about 143-147), and nothing links to them.
- Only they use these components: `review_step_bar`, `review_step_head`, `review_step_foot`, `review_step_done` and `review_source_head` (review_components.ex about 946-1275).
- `SourcePage`'s moduledoc describes the deleted overview.
- About 3,000 lines of tests drive the step URLs: the names, galls, disputes, hedges, trait_terms, step, question, source_text, titles, undo and facts tests (in `test/gallformers_web/live/admin/review_live/`).
- The dry run is written twice. `Review.preview`/`dry_run` (review.ex about 333-349) crashes on an error; item eb66 fixes the crash. `Facts.load_facts` (facts.ex about 113-132) handles errors and merges several packages.

**Jeff's decisions this rests on:**
- **(9, 2026-11-09) The Facts edits go on the review's last step.** Its Facts list (`facts_view`) gets the Facts page's per-Fact menu (Drop, Change Host, Change value, Note), with the shared `fact_editor` under the Fact and Undo under each edit. The review mockup doesn't draw these.
- **(2026-11-08, Jeff's answers on the test bed's affordances) "Facts edits: keep them, plainly."** Each statement on the last step gets "Change…" (Drop, Host, Values, Note). An edited one shows what changed and an Undo.
- **(2026-11-08, direction)** About 90% of edits to the system will come in through ingestion and be reviewed here. The Fact viewer and editor (87a1) is a separate tool for on-the-fly edits, and the two are not to be merged. So the review's own edits (the last step's statement edits) are built in the review, not deferred to the Fact editor.
- **(6611 build decision, 2026-11-08)** The Facts ("what approving will add") are the last step before Approve, by Gall, and reachable at any time from the rail.

**Build:**
- **First, the retirement entries.** Add `ReviewLive.Step` and `ReviewLive.Facts`, each replaced by `ReviewLive.Review`, to `test/support/retirements.exs` as the failing test (entries shaped `%{retire: Module, replaced_by: Module, matter: "id"}`). `Gallformers.Guardrails.Retirements` fails while both modules of an entry exist, and nothing else can be committed while an entry is outstanding. Delete the modules in the same commit, then remove the entries.
- **The Facts edits on the review's last step** (the `:facts` unit of `ReviewLive.Review`, drawn by `review_card` `:facts` with `facts_view`). Per Fact: Drop, Change Host (a plant of any rank, sections too), Change value (one or several of its Trait's values) and Note, through `fact_editor` and `Import.edit_fact/5`, each edit shown under its Fact with Undo (`Import.remove_fact_edit/2`). The Facts page (`ReviewLive.Facts`, events `edit_open`, `edit_save`, `edit_undo`, `search_plant`, `toggle_value`…) is the working reference for the behaviour. Its plant search goes through `GallformersWeb.Choices`, as item e7ef has it.
- **The dry run is `Facts.load_facts`'s,** with several packages merged and errors handled, replacing `Review.dry_run`. It runs in the background (`start_async`) after each edit, keeping what's shown in place. Running `Import.preview_package/2` takes up to a second on a large Source. Ordering against c90c's profiling of the dry run is an open question in the inbox.
- **Port the step and Facts tests' decision flows** to `question_card_test.exs` / `review_test.exs`. Then delete `Step`, `Facts`, the step components above and the step tests.
- **Redirect** `/admin/review/sources/:id/:step` and `/admin/review/packages/:package_id/:step` to the review with `?stage=<step>`, and the `/facts` URLs to `?facts` (the review's params: `?view=all`, `?stage=traits`, `?hedges`, `?facts`).
- **`GallformersWeb.Admin.Review.Steps` stays** (`Units` uses it). Fix `SourcePage`'s moduledoc.
- **Docs (from 6611 Task 10):** update `docs/design-system/patterns.md` (its "Review step" pattern), the style guide (drop the deleted components' entries) and their catalog entries in `components.md`. Lock the conformance gain in with `mix gallformers.design_system.baseline` (for gains only, never raised). 6611 Task 10 also says to delete the review mockup (`dev/gallformers_web/dev/mockups/source_reading_live.ex`) and its `/dev/mockups/source-reading/:package_id` route, since `mockups.md` says a built mockup goes. b230 says the mockups stay until Jeff has looked at the real UI. Whether this item deletes it is asked in the inbox. Don't delete it until that's answered.

**Deletes:** `ReviewLive.Step`, `ReviewLive.Facts`, `review_step_bar`, `review_step_head`, `review_step_foot`, `review_step_done`, `review_source_head`, `Review.dry_run`, the step and Facts routes (replaced by redirects) and the step tests (after porting their flows).
