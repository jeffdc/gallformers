The Source page (`/source/:id`, `lib/gallformers_web/live/source_live.ex`) contradicts itself once a Source is retracted.
- **"What this Source says"** (`facts_view`, from `Import.facts_for_source/1`) keeps listing the Source's Facts. Each Fact left with no other support carries a bare "retracted" mark: the raw standing atom, titled "This Fact's standing in Gallformers", from `fact_mark` in `lib/gallformers_web/components/facts_components.ex`.
- **The Galls list** ("Connected Galls"; now "Galls it covers") comes from `Catalog.list_galls_for_source/1`. That counts only Galls of **current** Facts the Source attributes (`Current.gall_ids_attributed_by/1`), plus legacy citations and Gallformers notes. So after a retraction, a Gall known only through this Source's Facts drops out of the Galls list while its Facts stay listed.

The two sections can disagree in general, not only after a retraction. Withdrawn, superseded and unsupported Facts are all listed, because FactsView carries every standing, but their Galls aren't counted.

Since the inventory, the page gained a Retracted chip and a danger notice with the date and a link to the notice Source (bc0d), so retraction itself is now visible. As of a0434bcf, `source_live.ex` still calls `facts_view` without its `history` option, which collapses non-current Facts. The Galls list is still current-Facts only. So the disagreement stands (read in code, not checked in a browser).

Details: `docs/design-system/inventory/host-and-source-pages.md`, "Findings" ("Retraction is invisible on the Source page").
