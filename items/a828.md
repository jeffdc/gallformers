Stored: alternate_generation_of is a Gall-to-Gall Relationship (RelationshipTypes :gall_to_gall; domain-model.md says alternate generations are stated on their own evidence, not inferred from a shared Inducer; PRODUCER_GUIDE.md and the import schema agree). Derived through the Species: the gall page's related_galls (catalog/read_models/gall_page.ex related_galls/2) lists other Galls with the same current species-rank Inducer, and the curator flags (catalog/read_models/flags.ex) raise :unlinked_generations for Galls sharing a species-level Inducer with different generations and no link, and :linked_inducers_differ for a link between Galls whose Inducers differ. The catalog migration creates no alternate_generation_of links, so today's alternate generations are implied only by a shared Inducer. To check: cases where the shared-species logic breaks, e.g. generations described under different names before the Taxonomy merges them, undescribed or unknown Inducers, genus-level Inducers, groups with more than two generations or host alternation (aphids), and Inducer synonymy or name changes.
