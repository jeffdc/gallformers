**The idea** (`86e7`, follow-ups after the name-builder build, 2026-10-27): "an imported Gall's `identified_by.plant_part` could become its form label (today such Galls get a ' (2)' override)."

**Background.** A Gall's name is derived from name parts stored on it (`86e7` §1): Inducer, form label, generation, Host qualifier, descriptor (undescribed Galls). The qualifiers come in the order form, generation, Host, e.g. "Neuroterus saltatorius (australis) (agamic)". Today's plant-part forms of one species are kept apart by their form label: "Asphondylia pseudorosa (bud)", "(capitulum)", "(leaf snap)", and "Tanaostigmodes howardii (detachable bud)" / "(integral stem)". Names are unique, case-folded.

**As of a0434bcf** (`Gallformers.Import.ApplyGall`): a new described Gall from a batch gets its Inducer, generation and a Host qualifier when the batch identifies it by a plant. It gets no form label. When the derived name is already shown, the Gall gets an override numbered " (2)", " (3)", …. So a second plant-part form of a species arriving from a Source is named "X y (2)" rather than "X y (bud)".

**What it would take.** In `ApplyGall`, when a new Gall's name collides with another Gall of the same Inducer that differs by `identified_by.plant_part`, set the form label from the plant part before falling back to numbering. Settle with Jeff: always, or only on a collision; and which wording, the contract's plant-part value or the Source's. Related: two of a batch's Galls with equal identifying fields are already a curator decision (`294c`, Jeff 2026-10-28), and Trait tiers in `identified_by` are deferred until it is measured how often that happens.
