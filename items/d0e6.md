**The bug** (`86e7`, follow-ups after the name-builder build, 2026-10-27): "if a curator sets an override while a rename proposal is open, the proposal stays open (accept refused, reject works)."

**Background** (`86e7` §3, "Renames are reviewed", Jeff decision B). A taxonomy change that would alter a Gall's derived name creates a `gall_rename` item in the admin queue instead of renaming the Gall. Such changes: the Inducer renamed, moved, re-ranked or merged; an "Unknown (Family)" Gall's family changing; a Host-qualifier taxon renamed. There is one open proposal per Gall, and the Gall keeps its name until a curator decides:
- **accept:** the new derived name applies, the old one becomes a `former_label` alias of the Gallformers Source, and the code is untouched;
- **reject:** the shown name becomes the Gall's `name_override`.

A Gall with an override gets no proposal, since its displayed name doesn't change. Deliberate edits to name parts or the override apply directly; they are the review.

**Still so as of a0434bcf** (from reading the code, not reproduced):
- `Gallformers.Catalog.update_name_parts/3`, the deliberate edit path, sets the override and recomputes `name` in one audited transaction. It does nothing about an open `gall_rename` item.
- `Gallformers.Import.GallRenames` withdraws an open proposal only when a later taxonomy change makes the name unchanged (`proposals/2` → `withdraw/1`).
- Accepting then fails: `Gallformers.Catalog.GallRenames.accept/2` rolls back with `:overridden`, and `Import.GallRenames` refuses the decision with "can't be accepted: the Gall has a name override (reject keeps it)". Rejecting works, and just re-sets the override to the name shown.

**Fix direction** (not decided): when a deliberate edit sets an override on a Gall with an open proposal, withdraw the proposal in the same transaction, since an override means no proposal. Or say on the queue item that the override has settled it. Keep the replay log consistent: proposals and their decisions replay (`GallRenames.replay_key/1`, `find/2`). Test first: override set while a proposal is open → no open proposal is left (or the item explains itself).
