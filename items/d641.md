`pending_trait_terms` is published in the public database snapshot, but its rows also hold IDs that point into private tables. Decide what the public copy should hold before cutover.

The table holds a Source's Trait term on a Gall that has no Fact yet: an ambiguous term's use, a disagreement between the readers, or a new term they didn't agree on, waiting on a curator's question. It is listed `public` in `priv/snapshot/tables.txt` ("Sources' Trait terms waiting on a question, shown on the Gall page (d213)"), because those terms show on Gall pages.

As of a0434bcf, each row (`Gallformers.Catalog.PendingTraitTerm`) keeps:
- `gall_id`, `source_id`, `trait`, `term`, `normalized_term`;
- `evidence` (the batch's quotes);
- `settled_at`, `settled_categories`, `settled_as`;
- and its origin: `package_id`, `batch_id`, `queue_item_id`, `statement_ref`.

`import_packages`, `import_batches`, `batch_statements` and `queue_items` are all `private` in the same file. The published IDs therefore point at nothing a snapshot user has, and they expose internal review structure.

Options:
- publish a derived or trimmed copy without the origin columns (the snapshot workflow already makes derived tables, `priv/snapshot/public_snapshot.sql`);
- keep the table whole, if the IDs are judged harmless;
- make it private, which loses the waiting terms that Gall pages show.

Also check whether `evidence` quotes are fine to publish under each Source's license. Stored Source text is private, and verbatim excerpts follow today's short-quote practice.

The snapshot workflow (`.github/workflows/db-snapshot.yml`) publishes from production's full backup, so this must be settled before cutover. `test/gallformers/public_snapshot_test.exs` only checks that every table is listed, not which columns are published.

priv/snapshot/tables.txt lists it as public (d213). From matter d213, 'Open / follow-ups' ("it also holds package and queue ids — check").
