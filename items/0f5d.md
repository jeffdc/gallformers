On 2026-11-05 Jeff said the ID tool needs a more substantial change in how it handles regions, to come back to after the design work (bc0d).

## The discrepancy

The two place reads (in code as of a0434bcf):
- **Known places.** Browse by place (`/places`) and the place page (`/place/:code`) count and list the Galls known in a place. They read `gall_index.known_place_ids`, computed by `Catalog.ReadModels.PotentialRange.known_for_galls/1`, through the Identify filter `:known_place_codes`. A Gall counts where:
  - its Hosts are native; or
  - a Host is introduced and the Gall itself is recorded in that country (a current recorded-occurrence Fact in the country or one of its subdivisions; a continent-level record shows none).

  A Gall with no range data is known nowhere.
- **The potential range.** The ID tool's region filter (`Gallformers.Catalog.Identify` `:place_codes`, used by `IDLive`) reads `gall_index.place_ids`. That is the Hosts' native and introduced ranges, as decided on 2026-10-25 (matter b5bd), plus every Gall with no range data, in every region (`g.everywhere`).

So the two can disagree. A North American oak gall whose only Host, red oak, is planted in Europe appears for Europe in the ID tool, but not on the Europe place page.

Jeff, 2026-11-05: counting introduced ranges was "wildly inaccurate". Also: "if a plant is introduced AND the gall is shown to be in the country then it should count", and "if the data is not present … the count is 0".

Only 4 recorded-occurrence Facts existed then, all for one Gall. So introduced ranges almost never count yet.

## To decide

- How the ID tool should treat regions: known places, the potential range, or something else.
- What to do with Galls that have no range data.
- How to show uncertainty.
- Whether the potential range keeps introduced ranges at all. This would revisit the b5bd 2026-10-25 decision and rule 10 of the domain design (matter 8567).
