AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md fall outside the allowlist, so they are always reviewed (reviewer.md's question 4 is about them). An empty file list doesn't skip. The allowlist is the one Jeff accepted; he may want more paths later (presentations' images already sit under docs/).
