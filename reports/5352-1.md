## How it was built

### Design notes

Approach: read-only SELECTs on gallformers_snapshot_20261113 (the newest production copy), exported to CSV, analysed with throwaway Python in docs/plans/5352-notes-analysis/ (gitignored, local). Notes were split into paragraphs and sentences (4,246 sentences) because about 30% of notes mix kinds; note-level labels alone would hide where code can and can't sort.

Measurement: a stratified random sample of 300 notes (176 undescribed, 124 described; 962 sentences) was labelled sentence by sentence into 13 content kinds, plus Jeff's hypothesis label per note, by four Claude agents working from a written guide (GUIDE.md) without keyword rules. No person labelled them; I spot-checked 40 labels (all defensible, about 4 arguable). Decision 6903 says "by hand"; 42bf corrects that. Population numbers are sample shares scaled to each stratum (about ±7 and ±9 points at 95%).

A keyword classifier (ordered regexes, one kind per sentence) was tuned on half the sample and scored on the other half, so the reliability figures on the page come from sentences it never saw: host additions 92/92 precision/recall, sightings 83/89, pointers 96/92; the prose kinds are much weaker (ID guidance recall 57%, curation 21%). Three routing schemes were scored against the labels: the hypothesis by flag (~73% of sentences), formulaic extraction plus flag (78%), the full classifier (80%). That small gap is the basis for recommending option C: let code do the formulaic kinds and route the rest by the Gall's undescribed flag, rather than trusting prose rules.

Also counted directly over all notes: host-addition parses checked against gallhost (165 of 175 match the Gall's hosts now; 10 don't), placeholder hosts (11), "not a gall" (10), notes on Hosts (2), legacy layouts, HTML markup, iNat Observation links (879 in 472 notes, relevant to 76f1).

Rejected: labelling all 1,330 notes with agents (a 300-note sample gives the proportions and an honest test of code); an interactive artifact (the item asks for a page Jeff marks up, and the 2026-10-28 sample set the convention of a local markdown file he edits).

New public functions, components, modules: none. Deleted: nothing. Unverified: the agents' labels beyond the 40 spot-checked; the classifier is a measuring tool, not the sorting code the Gallformers notes phase will write and test.
